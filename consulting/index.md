## What to Expect

1. **Choose an appointment.** Pick the session that best fits your question or
   project.
2. **Share the context.** Send any useful background so we can spend the call
   working on the problem.
3. **Leave with direction.** We will identify concrete decisions, next steps,
   and any follow-up work that would be useful.
