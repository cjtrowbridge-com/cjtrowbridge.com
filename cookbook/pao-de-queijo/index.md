### Ingredients

- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil, plus more for greasing the pan
- 2/3 cup milk
- 1 1/2 cups (170 g) tapioca flour
- 1/2 cup (66 g) packed grated or crumbled cheese, your preference
- 1 teaspoon salt (or more to taste)

## Special Tools

- Ideally, you would use something like an [aebelskiver](https://amzn.to/4hmhpRU) for this recipe, but something like a [cupcake/muffin pan](https://amzn.to/4wOmE1t) would also work.

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 400
- Oil inside each cupcake well
- Mix all ingredients and pour into the wells
- Cook 15-20 minutes
