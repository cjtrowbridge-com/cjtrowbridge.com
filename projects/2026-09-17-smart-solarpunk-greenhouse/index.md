The idea is a greenhouse that runs mostly on its own: solar in, food out, with a local model doing the slow decisions and a cheap microcontroller doing the fast ones. No cloud round-trips, no subscription, no phone required to keep the beggies alive. 

I'm also going to experiment with producing as much of the hardware myself as I can using 3d printed TPU for tools like ebb/flow beds, etc.

## Goals

- Produce all of my leafy greens and other veggies in this greenhouse 
- Track and automate it as much as makes sense
- Continue producing starter plants for the [Indigenous Solidarity Garden Project](https://www.gofundme.com/f/indigenous-solidarity-gardens-endangered-species)

## Parts List

### The Build

- [The Greenhouse](https://link.amazon/B0dj0aVcc) a cheap 5x5 transparent vinyl greenhouse.
- [Aluminet shade cloth](https://link.amazon/B08wwMofC) for plants that need it
- [USB Solar Panel](https://link.amazon/B0ir2euP4) this one supports PD so it can produce a lot more power than the normal ones. This will likely be an experimental power source that I will iterate over.
- [Cute solar fairy lights](https://link.amazon/B07WN70RY)

### Hydroponics

- [Hydroponic planter system](https://link.amazon/B09ndhm6n)
- [1.5" rockwool](https://link.amazon/B09kktJ8N)
- [Hydroponic nutrient solution](https://link.amazon/B0gG1RgvT)
- [USB water pump](https://link.amazon/B02NbaiQb)

I am going to be propotyping a TPU ebb/flow system which will initially be hydroponic and eventually aquaponic. I will be initially testing it with these super cheap tiny grow light beds to start some of the seeds for the larger hydroponics planter system.

- [Small one](https://link.amazon/B02Wn1m3X)
- [Big one](https://link.amazon/B0d31NFOv)

The big one will also be used for tree cloning experiments since it can maintain higher humidity while preparing the clones for dutch buckets

- [TPU filament](https://link.amazon/B0gAYKPb2)

Reputable TPU is the essential filament to use for 3d printing safe equipment that doesn't release toxic chemicals into your water, food, etc. 

### Soil Planting

For the [Indigenous Solidarity Garden Project](https://www.gofundme.com/f/indigenous-solidarity-gardens-endangered-species), I use [these planters](https://link.amazon/B07jFRVhQ) which are good at keeping the seeds humid while they get established, and then I can sticker them and give them to the Indigenous spiritual leaders.
